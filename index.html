Lorenz-96 / Independent Study Aug 2026 - Present

Lorenz-96 Subgrid Parameterization & Online Stability

Evaluating how much training data a neural network needs to parameterize unresolved subgrid coupling $U_k$ without drift or blow-up when integrated online in the single-layer Lorenz-96 model.

Monsoon Dynamics / ISI Kolkata (IDEAS-TIH) May 2026 - Jul 2026

Impact of ENSO on Kharif Crop Rainfall Variability

Summer project at ISI Kolkata building a spatial interpolation pipeline over IMD gridded precipitation data to increase resolution and calculate regional ENSO sensitivity indices across agricultural belts.
